03 · Connect a peer

No signaling server. You trade one blob out-of-band (Signal, in person, however you like). One of you creates an invite; the other answers; the first completes.

Advanced · NAT traversal (STUN)

Your browser asks this one box “what's my public address?” once, drops the answer into your invite blob, and it's out of the loop — it never relays a message. That public address is what lets a peer on another network reach you directly. Clear it to go same-LAN-only (host candidates); point it at your own coturn box to keep even the lookup in-house.

The 4th pillar implements Behavioral Entropy Signatures — Gary W. Floyd, Lumiea Systems Research Division · ThunderStruck Service LLC. Fallroom measures the behavioural domain only (keystroke & message cadence), builds each person's baseline online, and flags a rhythm that diverges from theirs — it never blocks a cryptographically valid message.
